Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 Seller / data controller: Aniket, operating as AEwheel Contact: support@aewheel.shop

This policy explains what data the AEwheel plugin and website collect, why, and your choices.

01Data the plugin collects and sends

To activate your licence and enforce the 2-device activation limit, the plugin sends the following to our server, which verifies it with Dodo Payments:

  • your licence key, when you activate it
  • a device identifier used to bind your activation and enforce the 2-device limit

The device identifier is a one-way hash of your machine's hardware ID. It cannot be reversed into your name, your email, or anything else that identifies you personally.

Nothing else leaves your machine. The plugin does not collect or transmit your After Effects projects, media, file contents, compositions, or any record of which tools you use inside the panel. There are no crash reports and no usage analytics of any kind. Your trial state and licence cache are stored locally on your machine.

On our server we store only the hashed device identifier, your trial start date, your licence key and activation records, and the email address attached to your order. We do not log your IP address or your browser details against your licence.

02Data our payment processor collects

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, acting as merchant of record. Dodo collects and processes your payment and billing information (email, payment details, tax data) to complete the sale, issue your licence, and handle any tax obligations. We receive limited order data — your email and licence status — to provide support and honour your licence. Dodo's handling of your data is governed by Dodo's own privacy policy.

03Data the website collects

This website runs no analytics and sets no tracking cookies. We do not profile visitors, and nothing you do on this site is tied back to you.

One thing worth stating plainly: the site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser fetches those font files directly from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request. That is the only third-party request the site makes. If you would rather avoid it, a content blocker will stop it and the site will fall back to your system fonts without breaking.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and the installer is downloaded from GitHub Releases. Both keep their own standard server logs, governed by their respective privacy policies.

04How we use data

  • to activate and validate your licence and enforce the device limit
  • to provide customer support
  • to email you about AEwheel if you contact support or buy a licence

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not send marketing email to people who have not contacted us or bought a licence.

05Third parties

  • Dodo Payments — checkout, payments, licensing, tax handling (merchant of record)
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and email forwarding for support@aewheel.shop
  • GitHub — hosting the installer download
  • Google Fonts — serving the site's typefaces, as described above

No other third party receives your data except as required by law.

06Data retention

Local files — your trial state and licence cache — remain on your machine until you uninstall AEwheel or delete them. Order records are retained by Dodo Payments, and by us, for as long as needed to support your licence and meet legal requirements.

07Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. Contact support@aewheel.shop for data we control, and contact Dodo Payments for billing data.

You can deactivate your licence on a device at any time using the panel's deactivate button, and uninstalling AEwheel removes its local data from that machine.

08Children

AEwheel is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

09Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change.
